Food after campus events

A voluntary two-minute survey for KSU students. Skip any question. We do not ask for names, emails, student IDs, phone numbers or precise locations. We report aggregate results with the sample size.

This is a student prototype, not an official KSU service or a promise of food. Please do not include identifying information.

Approval preview: no answers submitted or counted. The standalone web form is built at /survey; the public HTTPS link awaits deployment approval. Actual n=0.

1. Which campus are you usually on?
2. In the past month, how often did money or time lead you to skip a meal?
3. Have you seen leftover food from a campus event get thrown away?
4. Have you heard about free leftover food too late to get any?
5. Before this survey, had you heard of KSU CARE Services pantries?
6. Would you choose to receive alerts when free food is available nearby?
7. If you host events, what usually happens to leftovers?
8. Optional: What would make a leftover-food alert useful? Please do not include identifying information. Up to 500 characters. Leave blank to skip.

The deployed form stores no IP address or browser information with answers. Hosting providers may process standard technical logs. Raw responses are private; no public results page.
