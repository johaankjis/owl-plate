# SOUL.md

What Owl Plate is for and how it should feel. When a choice is unclear, decide in the direction this file points.

## The moment we exist for

A club meeting in the Marietta student center ends. Three trays of pizza and a bowl of wraps are left. The organizer is tired and the custodian is waiting. Today that food goes in the trash, or someone posts in a group chat and the first five people who see it take everything.

Meanwhile, across the building, a student skipped lunch because money is tight this week. They never see the post in time.

Owl Plate exists so the organizer takes one photo and walks away, and that student's phone buzzes.

## Who we serve

**Students who need a meal.** Many will never say so out loud. They should get food without proving anything, filling out forms or being seen as needy.

**Event hosts.** Clubs, departments and offices with leftovers. Their effort must be close to zero: one photo, one tap.

**CARE Services.** The KSU office already working on food insecurity. Owl Plate should feel like a tool they could adopt, not a competitor.

## Principles

**Need over speed.** Food should go to students who can use it, not just to whoever refreshes fastest.

**Just enough, not everyone.** Ping about as many students as there are servings. Thirty people racing for eight slices helps no one.

**Dignity by design.** Nobody can tell who was prioritized or why. Every alert looks the same. Pickup needs no proof.

**Safe before fast.** Never send food to someone it could hurt. Allergen estimates are honest estimates and say so.

**Zero effort for hosts.** If posting takes more than one photo and one tap, hosts will throw the food away instead.

**Real AI, honest limits.** The AI does real work (seeing the food, estimating servings, flagging allergens). We measure how accurate it is and say so.

**Built for KSU.** Two campuses, real buildings, CARE Services, meal swipe drives. Not a generic food app with a campus skin.

## Voice

Short, warm, practical. Never pitying.

* "Free food nearby: pizza and veggie wraps at the Student Center, room 214. About 8 servings. Tap if you're coming."
* "Your spot is held for 10 minutes."
* "This one has dairy, so we skipped it for you. More soon."
* To hosts: "Got it. About 8 servings. We'll send it to the right people."

No guilt, no charity language, no exclamation marks in alerts.

## What Owl Plate is not

* Not a broadcast channel. That already exists at other schools.
* Not a way to rank or label students.
* Not a food safety authority. Hosts follow KSU's food handling rules, and we remind them.
* Not a replacement for CARE Services or the pantries. It is another door into the help they already provide.

## The pitch in one breath

At KSU, leftover event food gets thrown away while students skip meals, and the free food alerts other schools use just reward whoever is fastest. Owl Plate takes one photo from the host, uses AI to see what the food is and how much there is, and sends it to just enough nearby students who can safely eat it, quietly putting students who need it most first.
