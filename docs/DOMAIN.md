## Existing free food programs (competitors)

| Program | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| UC Riverside, Scotty's Final Call | Text and email alerts about leftover food and supplies | UCR news, 2024 |
| CSUN MataBites | Notifications in the CSUN app, first come first served | CSUN |
| Cal State Fullerton Titan Bites | Push alerts; pickup window usually 15 to 20 minutes | Food Service Director |
| UC San Diego | Free food notifications in the campus app | UCSD Basic Needs |
| SF State Gator Grub Alert | Opt in channel in the campus app | SFSU Basic Needs |
| UT Austin UT Free Food | Opt in channel; 30 minutes to collect, first come first served | Daily Texan, 2025 |
| Pitt PittGrub | Alerts; pantry members more likely to be notified | Pittsburgh Magazine |
| FreeEats | Ready made app for any campus, .edu login, broadcast posts | Franklin University |
| Swipe Out Hunger | Meal swipe drives since 2010, now broader basic needs work | AJC partner article |

Our difference: all of these broadcast to everyone (food goes to the fastest) and require hosts to type details. Owl Plate uses one photo and AI to estimate the food, then dispatches to just enough nearby students who can safely eat it, with quiet prioritization.
