# DATA.md

All data Owl Plate needs for development, the demo and the pitch.

## 1. Buildings (`data/seed/buildings.yaml`)

The demo runs on the Marietta Campus (where the hackathon is held).

```yaml
- code: TODO(verify)
  name: "Joe Mack Wilson Student Center"   # Marietta pantry location per KSU Sentinel
  campus: marietta
  lat: TODO(verify)
  lng: TODO(verify)
  rooms: []
```

How to collect: open Google Maps, drop a pin on each main Marietta building entrance, copy lat and lng. 10 to 15 buildings is enough. Compute `h3_cell` at resolution 11 in the seed script, not by hand.

## 2. Simulated students (`data/seed/students_sim.yaml`)

About 30 simulated students so the map looks like a real campus. Generated by `make seed` with a fixed random seed:
* presence spread across buildings, weighted toward the student center and engineering buildings
* about 15% with one allergen (dairy, gluten, peanut most common), about 10% vegetarian
* about 15% CARE tier
* varied `claims_last_7d` and `ignored_last_7d`
* every record `is_simulated: true`, display names obviously fake ("Sim Student 07")

## 3. Demo personas (`data/seed/personas.yaml`)

Real phones, each logged in as one persona. Chosen so the demo shows every rule:

| Persona | Where | Diet / allergen | Tier | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone A | Same building as drop | none | none | Nearby gets invited |
| Phone B | Same building | dairy allergy | none | Skipped for allergen, stays silent |
| Phone C | Next building | none | CARE | Quietly prioritized, invited in wave 1 |
| Phone D | Far building | none | none | Not invited in wave 1, invited in wave 2 |
| Phone E | Same building | vegetarian | none | Invited only if drop tagged vegetarian |

## 4. Vision eval set (`data/eval/`)

We must be able to say how accurate the AI is.
* 20 to 30 photos of real food setups: pizza boxes, sandwich platters, cookie trays, wraps, fruit, mixed tables. Take them at club meetings, the dining hall, or set them up with grocery store platters.
* For each photo, a human writes `truth.json`: items, servings counted by hand, allergens from the packaging or menu.
* `make eval` reports: servings mean absolute error, percent of photos within plus or minus 2 servings, allergen recall (did we catch the allergens that were there), allergen false alarm rate.
* Put the real numbers on the "how it works" slide. Do not round up.

## 5. Survey (impact data for the rubric)

Campus impact is 25 points and must be backed by real data. Launch a Google Form on Wednesday in the AI Club Discord, class group chats and club channels. Close it Thursday evening.

Questions (keep it under 2 minutes):
1. Campus you're usually on (Kennesaw, Marietta, both)
2. In the past month, how often did you skip a meal because of money or time? (never, once or twice, weekly, more)
3. Have you seen leftover food from an event get thrown away? (yes, no)
4. Have you ever heard about free leftover food too late to get any? (yes, no)
5. Do you know about KSU CARE Services pantries? (yes, no)
6. Would you turn on alerts for free food nearby? (yes, maybe, no)
7. If you host events: what usually happens to leftovers? (thrown out, taken home, posted somewhere, other)
8. Optional: anything else about food on campus

Store raw exports in `data/survey/` (gitignored if any free text could identify someone). Report counts and percentages with the sample size on the slide ("n = 47").
